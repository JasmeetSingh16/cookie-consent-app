Privacy Policy — Cookie Consent Banner

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Cookie Consent Banner app ("the App") collects, uses, and protects data when installed on a Shopify store.

What data we collect

The App collects and stores the following information:

  • The shop domain of stores that install the App
  • Banner customization settings configured by the merchant (text, colors, position)
  • Anonymous consent events from storefront visitors: the choice made (accepted/rejected) and a timestamp

The App does not collect personally identifiable information about storefront visitors, such as names, emails, or IP addresses.

How we use data

Data is used solely to operate the App's functionality: displaying the correct banner settings to each store and maintaining a record of consent choices for the merchant's compliance purposes.

Data storage

Data is stored securely in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Data deletion

When a merchant uninstalls the App, all associated banner settings and consent event data for that store are automatically deleted within 48 hours, in compliance with Shopify's mandatory data protection requirements.
